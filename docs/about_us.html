About Us

Where Fragrance Becomes Presence

Sira is a fragrance brand created under Nira Industries, dedicated to crafting modern and long-lasting Eau de Parfums for everyday luxury. Our mission is to create fragrances that combine quality ingredients, elegant blends, and strong performance, allowing everyone to experience premium scents with confidence.


At Sira, we believe that fragrance is more than just a scent — it is a form of self-expression. Each perfume in our collection is carefully developed to deliver unique character, lasting freshness, and a memorable presence.


Backed by the experience and commitment of Nira Industries, we focus on quality, consistency, and innovation to bring refined fragrances to our customers.
