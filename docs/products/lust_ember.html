Vibe

Lust Ember is a warm, seductive fragrance that radiates passion and intensity. It is designed for those who carry a confident and irresistible presence. The scent feels bold, alluring, and slightly mysterious—perfect for evenings, dates, and special moments when you want to stand out. Lust Ember creates an atmosphere of attraction, warmth, and magnetic charm.

Fragrance Notes

  • Top Notes: Saffron, Pink Pepper
  • Heart Notes: Rose, Jasmine
  • Base Notes: Amber, Vanilla, Sandalwood

The fragrance opens with a spicy spark, unfolds into a rich floral heart, and settles into a warm, creamy base that feels sensual and captivating.

Longevity & Performance

  • Longevity: 8–10 Hours
  • Projection: Strong for the first 2–3 hours
  • Sillage: Leaves a rich and noticeable scent trail

Crafted with a high concentration of fragrance oils, Lust Ember delivers a bold and long-lasting performance that lingers beautifully on skin and clothing.
